译刻 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 8 日

一句话总结 译刻不收集、不上传你的任何数据。你的语音和文字只在你自己的 iPhone 上处理。

一、我们收集什么

不收集。

译刻没有账号系统，不需要注册或登录。我们不收集你的姓名、邮箱、电话、位置、设备标识或任何可以识别你身份的信息。

二、你的语音和文字去了哪里

只留在你的 iPhone 上。

  • 录音：仅在你按下 Action Button 后录制，停止后立即送入本机识别，录音文件用完即删。
  • 识别与翻译：全部由 iPhone 本地的模型完成，不联网、不上传。
  • 历史记录：原文、译文和时间保存在你的手机本地存储中。你可以随时在「设置 → 清除历史」里一次删掉全部记录。
  • 删除 App 时，上述本地数据会随 App 一并被系统删除。

三、权限说明

  • 麦克风：用来录制你要翻译的短句。不授权就无法使用录音功能。
  • 语音识别：仅在需要时请求；译刻默认使用本机识别，不依赖云端。

两个权限都只在本机使用，不会把你的音频发到任何服务器。

四、第三方

译刻不包含第三方广告 SDK、不包含第三方数据统计 SDK、不包含社交登录。App 内的语音朗读使用 iOS 系统自带的语音合成，同样在本地完成。

五、儿童

译刻不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会收集儿童的任何信息。

六、政策更新

如果未来译刻新增了需要联网的功能（例如账号或云端模型），我们会在发布前更新本政策，并在 App 内显著提示。用户始终可以选择不使用这类功能。

七、联系我们

对本政策有任何问题，请联系：j1yehang@163.com

Seylet — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

In one sentence Seylet collects nothing and uploads nothing. Your voice and text are processed entirely on your own iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. Seylet has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, device identifiers, or any personally identifying information.

Where your voice and text go

They stay on your iPhone.

  • Recording: recorded only after you press the Action Button, transcribed locally the moment you stop, and deleted right after.
  • Recognition and translation: performed entirely by on-device models. No network, no upload.
  • History: source text, translation and timestamp are stored locally on your device. You can erase everything at any time via Settings → Clear History.
  • Deleting the app removes that local data along with it.

Permissions

  • Microphone: used to record the phrase you want translated.
  • Speech recognition: Seylet uses on-device recognition by default and does not depend on the cloud.

Both permissions are used locally only. Your audio is never sent to any server.

Third parties

Seylet contains no third-party advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no social login. Spoken playback uses the built-in iOS speech synthesizer, which also runs locally.

Children

Seylet is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect information from children.

Changes

If a future version adds any networked feature (such as an account or a cloud model), we will update this policy before release and surface it clearly in the app. You will always be able to opt out of such features.
